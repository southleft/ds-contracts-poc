## The question

Every contract in `examples/<lib>/contracts` claims to be the output of the documented chain: a committed SEED plus a committed capture CONFIG, captured against the sandbox its `PROVENANCE.md` recreates, then promoted with the library's authored facts. Nothing checked that claim until 2026-08-24, and the first-pass exam found it false where it mattered most — `selftest-tailwind` stopped every one of its eight sets at `promote` because the committed capture record carried props no committed input produces (see [FIRST-PASS.md](FIRST-PASS.md)).

- **mui/tooltip** — THE CAPTURE REFUSES ITSELF — the sharpest row in this ledger, and an HONEST refusal by the engine's own determinism self-check. MUI's Tooltip mounts a Popper whose x offset is computed from the trigger's measured position, and two sweeps of the SAME configuration in one session disagree by 3px (19 vs 16). `extract/computed/run.ts` requires double-run byte-identity and stops. So `extract/computed/out/mui/tooltip` cannot be re-derived by anyone, on any machine, today — not because a fact was hand-written, but because the subject is not deterministic under the documented recipe. Cure: pin the Popper's placement in the capture config (a fixed anchor, the way the openDriver already pins `open`) so the offset stops being a function of layout timing, then recapture. Until then this record is a measurement nobody can repeat, and this row says so.
